# P1 offline diary

The pink + logs a real food on the selected diary day, or today from other tabs. Accept manually entered nutrition per 100 g or serving, portions to three decimal places, and a raw/cooked label. Keep the food label with the entry so totals survive edits to the reusable food. Show favorites first and recent foods next.

Use Room for foods and dated meal items. Group items by day and meal type. Keep personal targets and the existing demo preference in DataStore. Today can display clearly labeled samples when enabled in Settings. Diary and Insights always read local records.

Verify that a saved portion survives closing and reopening the app without networking, that editing updates totals, that repeated save commands insert once, and that deletion returns the day to its empty state.
